---
name: Facebook approvals and outcome evidence
description: Why group membership, publishing permission and browser confirmation must remain separate.
---

Approval to publish is not approval to request group membership. Membership is
also not proof that publishing is permitted. Keep these decisions separate.

**Why:** Combining them performs an additional account action the user did not
approve and can open membership questions or administrator review unexpectedly.

**How to apply:** Require separate reviewed join and post tasks. Never infer
successful membership from opening a join flow or a pending request.

A changed count of matching text in a Facebook feed is not reliable evidence
of a new post. Prefer a newly observed matching-text permalink in the approved
group; preserve uncertainty when the browser cannot establish that evidence.
Media-only submissions need manual review when no trustworthy receipt is found.

**Why:** Feeds can re-render or expose older identical posts. Moderator approval
and public visibility cannot be inferred from a browser submission.

**How to apply:** Do not weaken confirmation to dialog closure or text-count
changes. Surface uncertain native actions in the same module the user used,
including failures, cancellations and restarts, to prevent accidental reposting.
